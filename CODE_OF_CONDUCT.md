# Code of Conduct

This is a small project for people learning Redis. Everyone is welcome,
whatever their experience level.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone started somewhere.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Keep feedback about the code, not the person.
- Respect people's time: include versions and full errors when reporting a
  problem.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Publishing someone's private information
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the issue

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts
that break these rules. To report a problem, email **arsinghin@gmail.com**.
Reports are kept private.
